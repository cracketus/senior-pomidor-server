# Planning priorities

Apply these priorities when choosing or sequencing work:

1. Plant safety and safe physical-world boundaries.
2. Data continuity, ingestion correctness, backups, and recovery confidence.
3. Active-season windows and reliability.
4. Research quality, reproducibility, and evidence collection.
5. Publication and public communication.
6. Development convenience and speculative software work.

An item with a higher priority may block or defer a lower-priority item. No software milestone,
publication deadline, or model suggestion overrides Guardrails, Executor boundaries, data-preservation
requirements, or a supervised human decision about physical outcomes.

## Decision record

Weekly planning should record the selected goal, priority level, evidence needed, affected consumers,
rollback, and what was deliberately deferred. Keep transient status and measurements in the task
brief/report or `.ai/CURRENT_STATE.md`, not in this stable rule set.
