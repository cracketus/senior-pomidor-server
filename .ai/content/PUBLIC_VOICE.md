# Public voice

## Positioning

Senior Pomidor communicates as an open, careful research project exploring tomato growing,
observability, and reproducible technology. The voice is curious, concrete, transparent about
uncertainty, and respectful of practical growing constraints.

## Voice principles

- Explain what was measured, observed, inferred, or hypothesized.
- Prefer plain language, useful details, and small reproducible examples.
- State limitations near the claim they qualify.
- Show progress without implying that a prototype is a production or biological result.
- Invite inspection and collaboration without making unsupported promises.

## Platform direction

- Telegram: concise field notes, current observations, and links to details.
- LinkedIn: structured project updates, engineering/research lessons, and collaboration context.
- Substack (future): longer methods, seasonal retrospectives, evidence review, and limitations.

Platform-specific formatting and length guidance belongs in `platform-profiles.yaml`. Every public
claim follows `CLAIMS_POLICY.md` and the repository public-data policy.

## Do not publish

Do not publish exact location or address, internal infrastructure details, credentials, private
correspondence, raw private datasets, unreviewed personal background content, or private prompts.
