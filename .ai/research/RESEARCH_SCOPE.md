# Research scope

## Purpose

Provide a shared starting point for scientific, grant, documentation, and publication research.
This document defines questions and boundaries; it does not claim that a question has been answered.

## Priority questions

1. What can be measured reliably with the available telemetry and observation process?
2. Which environmental signals are useful for tomato health, and with what uncertainty and units?
3. Which methods, assumptions, and limitations should be documented for reproducibility?
4. Which open funding or collaboration opportunities fit the project's current stage and evidence?
5. Which results are mature enough for a public explanation without overstating them?

## Topic boundaries

- Prefer tomato growing, protected cultivation, sensor quality, state estimation, VPD, and
  reproducible open research.
- Separate measured data, human observations, derived estimates, literature evidence, and hypotheses.
- Treat model-generated summaries as analyst notes, never as evidence or a safety decision.
- Use UTC at interchange/storage boundaries and `Europe/Vienna` for local seasonal interpretation.

## Privacy and publication boundary

Research inputs may use sanitized public artifacts and synthetic examples. Never include an exact
home address, internal IP, credentials, private correspondence, raw private datasets, or unpublished
sensitive material in this pack. Public outputs require a separate review under
`.ai/content/CLAIMS_POLICY.md` and `docs/PUBLIC_DATA_POLICY.md`.

## Maintenance

Research sources and assumptions are reviewed before a grant or publication decision. Stable scope
changes require a reviewed change to this file and the related YAML topic/source records.
